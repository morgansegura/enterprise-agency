# Giving Tuesday and year-end campaign (2026)

**CVFC - Giving Tuesday and Year-End 2026**: November 17 to December 31, $40 a
day, sending donors to `/support`. Three ad groups: Giving Tuesday, Year-End
Giving and Local Giving. Built by `build-giving-campaign.py` into
`cvfc-giving-campaign.csv`; it imports paused.

While it runs, take its $40 from **CVFC - Neighborhoods** ($80 → $40), which
had no clicks in October. Put it back after December 31.

## Setting it up in Google Ads Editor

1. **Get recent account changes**.
2. **Account** → **Import** → **From file** → `cvfc-giving-campaign.csv`.
3. On the new campaign: **Start date** November 17, **End date** December 31.
4. Lower **CVFC - Neighborhoods** to $40 a day.
5. **Post**, then enable the campaign.

It counts donations through the **Donation** conversion, which showed
"Misconfigured" in Google Ads on November 9. Fix that before December 1.

Next year: change the dates in the headlines and descriptions, run the script,
and import again.
