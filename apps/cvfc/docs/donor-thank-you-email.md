# Donor thank-you email (Zeffy)

The message CVFC donors get after giving. It rides inside Zeffy's automatic
receipt email — the boxed "A thank you from…" section — so it reaches every
donor and carries the tax receipt with it.

**Where it lives:** Zeffy → Campaigns → Edit the donation form → **Thank you
email**. Insert the `First name` variable where the draft says `[First name]`,
and add the club logo above the signature.

**Reply-to:** set on that same screen. Point it at a club address
(`hector.diaz@chulavistafc.com` or `contact@chulavistafc.com`), never a personal
one — a donor replying to say thanks should reach the club.

**Voice:** "a club for everyone — the people's club" (the director's line).
Belonging first; scholarships and affordability are facts, not the identity.

---

## Subject line

Thank you for supporting Chula Vista FC

## Body

Hi [First name],

Thank you. Your gift goes straight to the field.

Chula Vista FC has been a Chula Vista club since 1982, and we're a club for
everyone — the people's club. Not a club for one kind of family, one
neighborhood, or one income. Every kid who wants to play belongs here.

Keeping it that way costs money. Your donation pays for player scholarships,
coaching, fields, and gear — the things that let a family say yes when a coach
says their kid is ready.

You're now part of that. We'll keep you posted on what your support makes
possible: the teams, the players, and the season ahead.

If you'd like to do more, the easiest thing is to tell one other person about
the club, or share our page with a South Bay family who's looking for a place
to play.

Passion. Unity. Respect. Attitude.

## Signature

Hector Diaz
Chula Vista FC
hector.diaz@chulavistafc.com

---

**Confirm before publishing:** who signs it. The draft uses Hector because his
address is the form's sender; the director or board president may be the better
voice for donors.
